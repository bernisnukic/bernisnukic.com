EditClips started with a simple idea: edit video clips in the browser without uploading them anywhere. It has grown into 150+ tools for converting, trimming, resizing, making GIFs, adding effects, cleaning up audio and editing photos.

Everyday edits run entirely on your device. FFmpeg is compiled to WebAssembly, so your files never leave your computer, and there's no sign-up and no watermark.

The heavier AI tools (background removal, license plate blur, frame interpolation, upscaling and generative inpainting) run on a self-hosted GPU rig that I build and maintain myself. They're free to try, with free credits every day.
