TotoNote is for anyone building a big fictional world. It's a rich-text editor where you can highlight any passage and tag it, and the highlight stays anchored to the right text as you keep writing. Sections and categories keep a large world navigable, and a graph view shows how everything connects.

Everything lives in a single SQLite database on your machine. There's no account and no cloud, and the dark, retro-terminal look is built to stay out of the way while you write.

Builds for macOS, Windows and Linux are on the releases page.
