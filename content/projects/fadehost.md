I founded FadeHost, a hosting company for the gaming industry, and I still build most of what runs it.

Today FadeHost focuses on Minecraft. Instead of paying for every server separately, you get one RAM pool and split it however you like: a survival server, a creative world, minigames and a proxy, all on one subscription. It comes with DDoS protection, instant setup and 24/7 support, and there's a 7-day free trial with no card.

Behind it is the website, the Laplace control panel customers use to manage their servers, and the automation that provisions and runs every server in Docker.
